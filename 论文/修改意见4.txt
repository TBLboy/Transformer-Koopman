原文：The modulation is parameterized with a total absolute budget that limits the magnitude of the context-dependent correction: the columns of $\mU$ and $\mV$ are reparameterized to have norm at most one, and $\gamma_\psi$ is reparameterized so that its components obey $\sum_{i=1}^{r}|\gamma_{\psi,i}(\vc)|\le g_{\max}$ for all $\vc$, their signs being unconstrained. Both are structural reparameterizations rather than training penalties. Concretely, the implementation sets $\gamma_{\psi,i}(\vc)=g_{\max}\,\pi_i\,\tanh([\mW_g\vc+\vb_g]_i)$, where $\pi$ is a learned simplex vector obtained by applying a softmax to a free parameter vector, so that the budget is attained by construction in every forward pass, $\sum_i|\gamma_{\psi,i}(\vc)|\le g_{\max}\sum_i\pi_i=g_{\max}$, and it normalizes the raw low-rank factors in every forward pass so that the columns of $\mU$ and $\mV$ have norm at most one; both constraints therefore hold structurally, with the parameter gradients flowing through the normalization. Writing $\mA(\vc)-\mA_0=\sum_{i=1}^{r}\gamma_{\psi,i}(\vc)\,\mU_{:,i}\mV_{:,i}^{\top}$ with columns of norm at most one, $\|\mU_{:,i}\|_2\le 1$ and $\|\mV_{:,i}\|_2\le 1$, the triangle inequality together with $\|\mU_{:,i}\mV_{:,i}^{\top}\|_2\le 1$ gives
\begin{equation}
    \|\mA(\vc)-\mA_0\|_2
    \le \sum_{i=1}^{r}\big|\gamma_{\psi,i}(\vc)\big|
    \le g_{\max},
    \label{eq:correction_bound}
\end{equation}
uniformly in $\vc$. This bounds the correction magnitude, not the stability of the resulting transition.
